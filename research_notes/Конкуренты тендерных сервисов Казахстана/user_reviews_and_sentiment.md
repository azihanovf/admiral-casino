# User reviews and sentiment: tender monitoring / tender-support services (Kazakhstan + Russian proxies), as of November 2026

> **Method and reliability note (read first).** In this session direct page fetching was blocked (every host — a2is.ru, startpack.ru, crmindex.ru, play.google.com — failed DNS resolution because it is outside the session's allowed network domains). All material below therefore comes from **search-engine result excerpts**, not from pages opened in full. Consequences:
> - Quotes in «ёлочки» are reproduced exactly as the fragment surfaced in search results (original spelling kept, including typos). They are fragments, not full reviews. Where only a paraphrase was available it is labelled *(paraphrase)* and is NOT verbatim.
> - Review dates were often not visible; marked *n/d* (no date). Where a date is given it is the one shown in the excerpt.
> - 2GIS, Google Maps, Yandex Maps, App Store, Google Play, Instagram, Telegram (t.me/s), YouTube and Threads could not be opened or paged through. Counts for 2GIS come from search excerpts of the cards and vary between cached versions.
> - **Honest bottom line on Kazakhstan:** independent review material for the Kazakhstan services is very thin. Only Tenderbot.kz, TenderPlus and Zakupki.kz have any third-party reviews at all, and most of those are about consulting/"tender under key"/training, not about the monitoring product. For MyTender, Tender Radar, SmartZakup, Smart Tender, Samruk Monitoring, AI TENDERR and Agashka **no independent user review was found anywhere**. The pain list therefore rests mainly on Russian proxies (Tenderplan, Rostender, Kontur.Zakupki, Bicotender, Seldon, TenderGuru), labelled **[PROXY-RU]**. Kazakhstan evidence is labelled **[KZ]**.
> - Source types: **IND** = independent third-party review platform; **SELF** = published by the service itself; **ED** = editorial / listing / likely advertorial.

## 1. What do users complain about (noise, missed tenders, lateness, keyword search, coverage, price, billing, auto-renewal/refunds, support, AI, interface)?

### Takeaway
Across the category the most repeated complaints are (a) keyword/filter setup that users cannot do without a manager, (b) notification noise and false or stale alerts, (c) tenders that exist on the official portal but are missing or arrive hours late, (d) price for small firms plus billing surprises, (e) pushy sales calls, and (f) support limited to business hours. Direct Kazakhstan evidence exists for only three of these (one email per lot, "nothing is clear with this bot", paid-for hope sold to the naive); everything else is proxy evidence from Russian services. No user complaint specifically about annual-only billing or about AI analysis quality was found.

### Inferences
Consolidated list of concrete user pains for this product category (evidence strength in brackets):
1. **Keyword/filter setup requires a human.** Users say they never learned to configure keys and ask the manager every time. [PROXY-RU strong: Tenderplan ×4, Kontur, Bicotender; KZ weak: "Ничего не понятно с этим ботом"]
2. **Notification noise.** Too many alerts at the start, false "change" alerts, one message per lot instead of one per tender, expired tenders left in results. [PROXY-RU medium; KZ: one 2019 TenderPlus review]
3. **Missed or late tenders versus the official portal.** Tenders visible on the state portal are absent or arrive hours to a day late; support blames the state portal load. [PROXY-RU medium]
4. **Incomplete platform coverage.** "Not all platforms", niche auction types missing, weak for local/state tenders in one case. [PROXY-RU medium]
5. **Price is high for small suppliers and often not published.** Entry prices quoted per year; pricing only via a manager; extra charges discovered after payment. [PROXY-RU medium]
6. **Billing surprises and hard exits.** Bills and a debt letter arriving after the user stopped using the service, invoice-only renewal with no card payment, no subscription pause, refunds refused for "we will win it for you" services. [PROXY-RU medium; the Rostender 2GIS 2025 case is the single clearest one]
7. **Pushy sales.** Multiple calls per day from different numbers right after registration; scripted managers. [PROXY-RU strong: Kontur, Rostender, Seldon]
8. **Support only in business hours, slow or scripted, bug reports ignored.** [PROXY-RU medium]
9. **Dated or over-complicated interface; slowdowns with many filters.** [PROXY-RU medium: TenderGuru, Bicotender, Tenderplan, Rostender]
10. **Documents handled poorly.** Not all files in-app, must download to view, no search inside documentation. [PROXY-RU medium]
11. **Result promises not kept in "tender under key" support services.** [PROXY-RU: Bicotender; KZ: one Tenderbot late-submission case]
- Annual-only billing and auto-renewal as named complaints did **not** appear in user reviews; the closest evidence is the Rostender unexpected-debt case and per-year price anchors (Kontur from 21 240 ₽/year, Seldon about 100 000 ₽/year).
- The Russian B2B norm is invoice/contract billing, so "auto-renewal" complaints of the consumer-subscription kind are structurally rarer there; a Kazakhstan service charging cards monthly (e.g. 9 990 ₸/month tariffs at Tender Radar and MyTender) may face this complaint type without precedent in the proxies.

### Gaps
- No independent review text could be read for any Kazakhstan service on Google Maps, Yandex Maps, App Store, Google Play, Instagram, Telegram, YouTube or Threads; these hosts were not reachable and search returned nothing from them.
- 2GIS low-star reviews for Tenderbot (96 reviews) and TenderPlus could not be sorted or read in full, so negative Kazakhstan reviews may exist that are not captured here.
- No user complaint specifically about annual-only billing, auto-renewal of a card subscription, or wrong/shallow AI analysis was found for any service.
- Most proxy quotes are undated in the excerpts; the dated ones span 2019–2025. Freshness of individual complaints is uncertain.

## 2. What do users praise and say they actually pay for?

### Takeaway
Users pay for not missing relevant tenders (timely alerts across many platforms in one place), for time saved, and for a human who sets the search up and answers questions. In Kazakhstan the praise that exists is mostly for the personal manager and for consulting, training and "tender under key" services rather than for software features.

### Inferences
- Independent review volume in Kazakhstan is roughly two orders of magnitude lower than for Russian proxies when summed across platforms; the market's public reputation layer is essentially unbuilt.
- High catalogue averages for the Russian proxies coexist with sharp negative reviews on hf.ru, 2GIS and pravda-klientov; catalogue averages (startpack, a2is, crmindex) likely skew positive because vendors solicit reviews there.
- Kontur.Zakupki's 5.9 of 10 on hf.ru against otherwise positive catalogues shows the largest brand also draws the most sales-pressure and service complaints.

### Gaps
- No App Store or Google Play listing with ratings was found for any Kazakhstan service; MyTender describes itself as "Web, Android, iOS (PWA)", which suggests no native store app, but this was not verified in the stores.
- Google Maps and Yandex Maps ratings were not obtainable for any service.
- Star averages for several Russian platforms (startpack, crmindex, hf.ru for Tenderplan/Rostender) were not shown in excerpts; only counts are reported.
- 2GIS counts differ between cached excerpts (for example Tenderbot 104/109/113 ratings; Rostender 28–59), so treat all 2GIS numbers as approximate as of late 2026.
