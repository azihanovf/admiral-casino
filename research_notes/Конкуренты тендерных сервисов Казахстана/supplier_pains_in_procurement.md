## Objective: ranked, evidence-backed list of supplier pains across the whole journey

### Takeaway
The best-evidenced pains in 2025–2026 are (1) bids rejected on formal/technical-specification grounds and competitors wrongly admitted, (2) late payment and acceptance-act friction, (3) the two-year blacklist risk (РНУ) for a single slip, and (4) restrictive or badly written technical specifications — all four are named by the Atameken council in September 2026 and are visible in complaint and court statistics. Roughly half of these pains (document checking, eligibility pre-check, deadline control, keeping up with rule changes, finding viable lots) are of the kind software/AI can reduce; the other half (customer non-payment, tailored specs, blacklist law, frozen money, tax/experience thresholds) are structural.

### Cited Findings
- Atameken's Council for the Protection of Entrepreneurs' Rights and Anti-Corruption (chaired by Kairbek Suleimenov; meeting reported as held 25 Sept 2026, publications 28 Sept 2026) listed as the most common problems: rejection of bids on formal grounds, requirements restricting competition, incorrectly drafted technical specifications, wrong evaluation criteria, violation of procurement deadlines/procedure, unjustified bundling of heterogeneous goods into one lot, late payment for performed contracts, late return of contract-performance security, and inclusion of entrepreneurs in the register of unscrupulous participants — [inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/ru/last/atameken-nazval-glavnye-problemy-goszakupok-dlya-biznesa); [nomad.su](https://nomad.su/?a=3-202609280031); [cdb.kz](https://cdb.kz/sistema/novosti/npp_atameken_problemy_primeneniya_novogo_zakonodatelstva_o_goszakupkakh/)
- Same meeting: in H1 2026 about 3 thousand administrative cases on procurement violations were opened and 2,942 persons were held administratively liable; civil court cases on public procurement grew by 30% in 2025; entrepreneurs' claims were found justified in 22% of cases (nomad.su adds: 18–22% after the transition to administrative justice) — [cdb.kz](https://cdb.kz/sistema/novosti/npp_atameken_problemy_primeneniya_novogo_zakonodatelstva_o_goszakupkakh/); [inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/ru/last/atameken-nazval-glavnye-problemy-goszakupok-dlya-biznesa); [nomad.su](https://nomad.su/?a=3-202609280031)
- Market size context: Minfin figures cited via KazTAG (30 Jan 2026): public procurement volume 8.1 trn tenge, 18 thousand customers, 120 thousand active suppliers; duna.kz estimates only ~5.9% of active legal entities and IEs take part, average contract ≈ 4.9 mln tenge — [duna.kz](https://duna.kz/research/kazakhstan/b2b-prodazhi-i-tendery-goszakupki-kazakhstan-2026)
- Deputies put the combined state + quasi-state + regulated procurement market at 29–30 trn tenge (about a quarter of GDP) with ~300 thousand micro and small businesses acting as suppliers — [kz.kursiv.media, 18 Mar 2026](https://kz.kursiv.media/2026-03-18/fvfv-deputaty-protiv-popravok-kotorye-zakroyut-300-tys-kompaniy-dostup-k-goszakupkam/)

## Why are supplier applications rejected most often? Rejection and failed-procurement statistics

### Takeaway
Rejections cluster around documents and technical-specification compliance rather than price: mismatched or sloppy descriptions of goods, missing licences/permits, wrong or expired forms, security errors and upload mistakes; on the customer side, commissions also reject on grounds not present in the documentation. Complaints about wrongful admission of competitors and wrongful rejection are the top complaint category on the portal, and a large share of lots fail outright.

### Cited Findings
- In January–August 2025, 19.9 thousand participant complaints were posted on the portal; about 6 thousand were satisfied in full, 71 in part, about 13.6 thousand refused. Most frequent subjects: wrongful admission of competitors, incorrect rejection of a bid, inflated or unjustified requirements of organisers. Fragment: «Всего за восемь месяцев 2025-го на портале было размещено 19,9 тыс. жалоб участников. Из них только 6 тыс. жалоб были удовлетворены полностью, а 71 жалоба — частично.» / «Чаще всего предприниматели жаловались на неправомерный допуск конкурентов к участию в закупке, неверное отклонение заявки на участие, завышенные или необоснованные требования организаторов.» (fragments) — [finprom.kz](https://finprom.kz/ru/article/kazhdyj-desyatyj-tenge-v-goszakupkah-tratitsya-s-narusheniyami-i-tak-iz-goda-v-god). Note: a second search rendering of the same article garbled "жалоб" as "процедур"; verify on the page.
- The portal's cumulative complaint register (all years, not 2025) shows «Рассмотрена – удовлетворена: 90082; 47.53%» (fragment) — [old.goszakup.gov.kz complaint register](https://old.goszakup.gov.kz/ru/registry/complaint)
- Selective cameral control: roughly every tenth tenge in procurement is spent with violations (10.5% in 2023, 10.6% in 2022 per the article); checked procedures fell 19% year-on-year and procedures with violations halved in 8 months of 2025 — [finprom.kz](https://finprom.kz/ru/article/kazhdyj-desyatyj-tenge-v-goszakupkah-tratitsya-s-narusheniyami-i-tak-iz-goda-v-god); [inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/ru/last/kontrol-goszakupok-proverok-menshe-a-summa-narushenij-bolshe)
- Typical supplier-side errors described by a tender service: mistakes in the description of product characteristics, actual parameters not matching the tender requirements, typos in product names, missing licences/certificates, wrongly filled forms, expired documents, missing ЭЦП where required, wrong file name, errors in the bank guarantee, file-upload errors and last-minute submission, wrong VAT calculation and un-costed transport/duties — [tenderbot.kz](https://tenderbot.kz/blog/post/tipichnye-oshibki-pri-uchastii-v-tenderakh-i-kak-ikh-izbezhat)
- A bid is rejected if the supplier did not confirm qualification requirements or attach required permits/licences; one of the most common commission errors is rejecting on grounds that were not in the procurement documentation; grammatical or arithmetic errors that can be corrected without changing the substance should not lead to rejection — [gz.mcfr.kz](https://gz.mcfr.kz/article/530--sluchai-nesootvetstviya-potentsialnogo-postavshchika-kvalifikatsionnym-trebovaniyam)
- Law firm framing of the same problem (rejected-bid service page) — [sdlegalpro.kz](https://sdlegalpro.kz/otklonili-zayavku-goszakupki-kazahstan/)
- Some lots require licences available only to legal entities, so IE bids are rejected (blog claim, not a statistic) — [gz.mcfr.kz, "ТОО или ИП"](https://gz.mcfr.kz/article/1411-too-ili-ip-chto-luchshe-vybrat-dlya-uchastiya-v-goszakupkah-rk)
- Failed lots: over a rolling 30-day window (page updated roughly late Oct 2026) about 55.7% of goszakup lots ended without a result, 44% of procedures that did receive bids had a single participant, and the median winning discount was 8.3% — [mytender.kz](https://mytender.kz/guide/kak-uchastvovat-v-goszakupkah) (commercial service's own calculation, not official)
- Under the Rules (version of 1 Jan 2026), if fewer than two price offers remain after the portal's automatic rejection, the procurement is declared failed — [pro1c.kz text of the Rules](https://pro1c.kz/articles/prochee/pravila-gosudarstvennykh-zakupok/)
- New automatic rejection rule: if at least two private suppliers bid, the portal automatically rejects bids of state enterprises (and, per MyTender, companies with ≥50% state share); a Prime Minister's letter of 25 Apr 2026 addresses this — [prg.kz](https://prg.kz/m/amp/document/35520090/2/80000); [mytender.kz](https://mytender.kz/guide/kak-uchastvovat-v-goszakupkah)
- Older international assessment (World Bank/OECD/EBRD, about 2019): among constraints, "inadequate capacity of the private sector to prepare quality and responsive bids" — [World Bank assessment](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/564591589875649239/pdf/Assessment-of-the-Public-Procurement-systems-of-the-Republic-of-Kazakhstan-and-the-Sovereign-Wealth-Fund-Samruk-Kazyna.pdf)

### Inferences
- About 30% of 2025 portal complaints were fully satisfied (6k of 19.9k). That means thousands of rejections/admissions per year are officially recognised as wrong — and that roughly two thirds of complaining suppliers were themselves mistaken or could not prove their case. Both halves point to a document-checking need.
- The supplier-side error list (spec mismatch, expired document, missing permit, wrong form, upload error) is exactly the class of error a pre-submission checker can catch; arbitrary rejections "on grounds not in the documentation" are not.
- A 55.7% no-result share suggests a great deal of supplier effort goes into lots that never produce a contract; filtering lots by viability is a distinct, software-addressable pain.

### Gaps
- No official breakdown of rejections by reason (formal error vs. spec vs. qualification vs. experience vs. permits) was found; only qualitative lists.
- No official 2025/2026 figure for the share of failed procurements was found. The goszakup statistics page shows a line "из одного источника по несостоявшимся закупкам — 436 855; 25,67" for 2026, but the meaning of 25.67 is unclear from the fragment ([duna.kz search rendering](https://duna.kz/research/kazakhstan/b2b-prodazhi-i-tendery-goszakupki-kazakhstan-2026)); treat as unconfirmed.
- The MyTender 55.7% / 44% figures are a vendor's rolling calculation and were not cross-checked.

## How common are tailored technical specifications; how do suppliers detect and contest them; discussion stage, complaints, deadlines

### Takeaway
Restrictive or incorrect specifications and lot bundling are officially acknowledged as among the most common violations, but no figure for how often specs are tailored was found. Contesting is hard: the complaint window is three working days after the results protocol, complaints now go to the organiser itself, rating-point contests cannot be appealed pre-trial, and court success is low.

### Cited Findings
- Atameken council (Sept 2026): restricting requirements, incorrectly drafted technical specifications, wrong evaluation criteria and bundling heterogeneous goods in one lot are among the most widespread violations in organising procurement — [cdb.kz](https://cdb.kz/sistema/novosti/npp_atameken_problemy_primeneniya_novogo_zakonodatelstva_o_goszakupkakh/); [inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/ru/last/atameken-nazval-glavnye-problemy-goszakupok-dlya-biznesa)
- "Inflated or unjustified requirements of organisers" is one of the three most frequent complaint subjects on the portal (Jan–Aug 2025) — [finprom.kz](https://finprom.kz/ru/article/kazhdyj-desyatyj-tenge-v-goszakupkah-tratitsya-s-narusheniyami-i-tak-iz-goda-v-god)
- Customers sometimes set requirements without functional necessity: «Такие требования могут привести к ограничению конкуренции, увеличить стоимость закупки, стать основанием для жалобы.» (fragment); a badly drafted specification can lead to cancellation of results — [tenderbot.kz](https://tenderbot.kz/blog/post/oshibki-zakazchikov-v-tehnicheskoj-specifikacii)
- Supplier voice from a preliminary-discussion thread on the portal (date not visible): «Между тем техническая спецификация закупаемых товаров по установленной форме заполняется заказчиком.» (fragment; a supplier demanding removal of unlawful conditions from the tender documentation) — [goszakup.gov.kz discussion page](https://goszakup.gov.kz/ru/announce/actionShowDiscus/16910389/629933)
- Example complaint ground against a competitor: «ТОО «ААА» не является авторизованным партнёром правообладателей решений и не имеет права на их поставку» (fragment, illustrative example in a guide) — [gz.mcfr.kz](https://gz.mcfr.kz/article/335-jaloby-goszakupki)
- Complaint deadline: under Article 25 of Law No. 106-VIII a potential supplier files via the web portal within three working days after the protocol on the results of the contest/auction is posted — [kodeksy-kz.com, art. 25](https://kodeksy-kz.com/ka/o_gosudarstvennyh_zakupkah/25.htm); [gz.mcfr.kz](https://gz.mcfr.kz/article/335-jaloby-goszakupki); [korgan-zan.kz](https://korgan-zan.kz/spory-po-goszakupkam-otstranenie-i-otmena-itogov/)
- Since 1 Jan 2025 complaints are considered by the organiser of the procurement, not by the authorised body; appeal is via the portal and only where the winner is chosen by a tender commission — [cdb.kz](https://cdb.kz/sistema/novosti/s_1_yanvarya_2025_goda_novyy_zakon_rk_o_gosudarstvennykh_zakupkakh/)
- In contests using the automated rating-point system there is no pre-trial appeal of results, which a law firm expects to increase court disputes — [unicaselaw.com.kz](https://unicaselaw.com.kz/amendments-in-kazakhstan-public-procurement-legislationsk-lang-more-rus); confirmed by the Rules text (results not appealable under art. 25) — [pro1c.kz](https://pro1c.kz/articles/prochee/pravila-gosudarstvennykh-zakupok/)
- Vice-minister of finance Dauren Kenbeil: «Конкурсная комиссия не создается, то есть нет человеческого фактора. Используя наши источники, победитель будет определен автоматически и итоги конкурса по этой системе обжалованию не подлежат» (fragment) — [bizmedia.kz, 17 Apr 2025](https://bizmedia.kz/2025-04-17-zakon-o-goszakupkah-v-kazahstane-kak-on-otrazitsya-na-biznese-i-podryadchikah/)
- Effect on complaint volume: an internal state audit department in Kostanay region said complaints fell by about a third since the start of 2025 and linked it to the new law and the changed appeal procedure; 1,918 notices to remedy violations were sent in 2024 after cameral control — [ng.kz](https://ng.kz/modules/news/article.php?storyid=55386)
- Courts: in H1 2025, of 572 administrative cases considered, the claimant won in 301 (52.6%); procurement disputes were up 54% on H1 2024; 614 cases concerned disputes arising before contract signing (+41.5%) — [ranking.kz](https://ranking.kz/reviews/industries/narusheniya-v-sfere-goszakupok-kolichestvo-sudebnyh-del-vyroslo-v-poltora-raza.html); [inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/ru/last/tysyachi-del-v-kazahstane-rezko-uvelichilis-spory-v-goszakupkah); [kapital.kz](https://kapital.kz/economic/139736/narusheniya-v-sfere-goszakupok-kolichestvo-sudebnyh-del-vyroslo-v-15-raza.html)
- Business ombudsman Kanat Nurov (podcast, reported 5 May 2026) said procurement holds back the economy and advised entrepreneurs not to waste time contesting tender results in court because they will lose time and money on court costs — [kz.kursiv.media](https://kz.kursiv.media/2026-05-05/svan-biznesmenam-luchshe-ne-svyazyvatsya-s-goszakupkami-biznes-ombudsmen-kanat-nurov/)
- Since 12 July 2026 (quasi-state rules, order No. 297 of 8 May 2026): after the results protocol participants can view other suppliers' bids except price offers; price offers are confidential until results — [zakon.kz, 15 May 2026](https://www.zakon.kz/pravo/6517963-izmenilis-pravila-goszakupok-predstavitelyami-kvizigosudarstvennogo-sektora.html); [inform.kz](https://www.inform.kz/ru/minfin-rkizmenil-pravila-goszakupok-dlyakvazigossektora-d1438510)
- Order No. 102 of 13 Feb 2026 (in force 27 Feb 2026) changed, among other things, the procedure for preliminary discussion of tender documentation in the quasi-state sector — [prg.kz](https://prg.kz/m/amp/document/34553751/3/7600)
- Subsoil-user procurement: entrepreneurs name expensive certification, non-transparent tenders and requirements that may be oriented to specific suppliers as barriers — [24.kz](https://24.kz/ru/news/economyc/788244-neftegazovye-operatory-dolzhny-izmenit-podkhody-k-zakupkam)
- Samruk-Kazyna: complaints go through the portal cabinet or eOtinish to the organiser or the Fund's procurement control department; review takes 20 working days; they are handled under the Fund's internal rules, not the public procurement law — [tenderplus.kz](https://tenderplus.kz/blog/samruk-kazyna-kak-obzhalovat-itogi); [yur.kz](https://yur.kz/articles/zakupki-samruk-kazyna-chem-otlichayutsya-ot-goszakupok/)
- OECD anti-corruption monitoring (baseline report, 2024): grounds for single-source procurement remained overly broad; direct contracts exceeded 30% of purchasing in 2023 in each monitored sector — [OECD baseline report](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2024/11/baseline-report-of-the-fifth-round-of-monitoring-of-anti-corruption-reforms-in-kazakhstan_92cefea2/c9652173-en.pdf)

### Inferences
- A three-working-day window, with the organiser judging complaints about itself, makes speed of reading and drafting the binding constraint. Detecting restrictive clauses during the discussion stage and drafting a remark or complaint is a narrow but real AI use case; whether the complaint succeeds is structural.
- Court odds look very different by metric (52.6% claimant wins in administrative cases in H1 2025 vs. 22% "entrepreneurs' claims justified" per Atameken for 2025). These measure different populations (claimants include customers and state bodies), so they should not be merged.
- The new right to see competitors' bids after the protocol (quasi-state, from 12 Jul 2026) creates a fresh document-reading task inside a short appeal window.

### Gaps
- No statistic on how often specifications are tailored to a supplier was found.
- Exact current deadlines of the preliminary-discussion stage (days for remarks, days for the organiser's reply, how the discussion protocol is appealed) under the 2026 version of Rules No. 687 could not be confirmed — the query was blocked by the search budget. Verify in chapter on preliminary discussion at [adilet.zan.kz](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2400035238).
- One legal blog gives "5 working days" for complaints, conflicting with the three-working-day rule in art. 25 ([femida-justice.com](https://femida-justice.com/blog/spory-po-goszakupkam-v-kazahstane-sudebnoe-obzhalovanie-i-zashhita-postavshhika/)); likely refers to a different procedure — unverified.
- Anti-Corruption Agency material on "заточка" schemes was not found; the Agency was reorganised into an Anti-Corruption Service under the National Security Committee in mid-2025 according to [czhr.kz](https://www.czhr.kz/post/%D0%B0%D0%BD%D1%82%D0%B8%D0%BA%D0%BE%D1%80-%D0%BF%D1%80%D0%BE%D1%81%D1%83%D1%89%D0%B5%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B2%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0%D0%BB-%D0%BA%D0%B0%D0%BA-%D1%81%D0%B0%D0%BC%D0%BE%D1%81%D1%82%D0%BE%D1%8F%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BB%D1%8C%D0%BD%D0%B0%D1%8F-%D1%81%D1%82%D1%80%D1%83%D0%BA%D1%82%D1%83%D1%80%D0%B0-6-%D0%BB%D0%B5%D1%82) (dates in the snippet were inconsistent).

## Dumping and anti-dumping security, bid security via the electronic wallet, bank guarantees: money frozen and typical problems

### Takeaway
Suppliers' money is blocked at three points — bid security, contract-performance security and, for low prices, an anti-dumping sum equal to the gap between the dumping threshold and the offered price — and it stays blocked until the contract is fully performed (and is forfeited on termination for non-performance). Late return of performance security is an officially acknowledged, persisting problem; dumping thresholds differ by procurement type and sources disagree on them.

### Cited Findings
- The single operator blocks the performance security and anti-dumping sum on the supplier's electronic wallet and returns them within three working days after full performance: «Единый оператор автоматически разблокирует и осуществляет возврат на электронный кошелек поставщика заблокированное им обеспечение исполнения договора, антидемпинговую сумму (при наличии), в течение 3 (трех) рабочих дней» (fragment of the Rules); the sum is not returned if the contract is terminated for non-performance — [adilet.zan.kz, Rules](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2400035238); [pro1c.kz](https://pro1c.kz/articles/prochee/pravila-gosudarstvennykh-zakupok/)
- If the price is found to be dumping, the supplier must post anti-dumping security equal to the difference between the dumping threshold price and the offered price; one source gives 10 working days from contract conclusion to post it; it can be posted via e-wallet or electronic bank guarantee, not via an insurance contract — [tenderbot.kz](https://tenderbot.kz/blog/post/kak-postavshchiku-vnesti-antidempingovuyu-summu-v-goszakupkakh); [tenderbot.kz, types of security](https://tenderbot.kz/blog/post/vidy-obespecheniy-v-gosudarstvennykh-zakupkakh)
- Dumping thresholds reported (conflicting, by procurement type): price more than 10% below the allocated amount (general / request for quotations; allowed only with an anti-dumping sum) — [mytender.kz](https://mytender.kz/guide/sposoby-goszakupok); more than 20% below the allocated amount for current repairs from 1 Jan 2026 — [gz.mcfr.kz](https://gz.mcfr.kz/article/1272-pravila-gosudarstvennyh-zakupok-v-kazahstane); more than 2% below the estimate for construction works, dumping not admitted — [duna.kz](https://duna.kz/research/kazakhstan/b2b-prodazhi-i-tendery-goszakupki-kazakhstan-2026); e-shop: more than 30% below the average of offers is automatically rejected — [mytender.kz](https://mytender.kz/guide/sposoby-goszakupok); 50% below the arithmetic mean of admitted bids — [femida-justice.com](https://femida-justice.com/materialy/goszakupki-po-novomu-kak-rabotat-po-zakonu-2025-goda/kak-rabotaet-demping-v-goszakupkax-zashhita-ot-sverxnizkix-czen/)
- Atameken council, Sept 2026: the problem of untimely return of contract-performance security persists; the General Prosecutor's Office was recommended to strengthen oversight of payment and security-return deadlines — [cdb.kz](https://cdb.kz/sistema/novosti/npp_atameken_problemy_primeneniya_novogo_zakonodatelstva_o_goszakupkakh/); [inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/ru/last/atameken-nazval-glavnye-problemy-goszakupok-dlya-biznesa)
- A Treasury Committee letter of 12 Feb 2026 (No. ЖТ-2026-00293108) is titled "On violation of the deadlines for posting contract-performance security" — an indicator that missed security deadlines are a recurring issue — [prg.kz](https://prg.kz/document/?doc_id=35281636)
- Errors in the bank guarantee are listed among causes of bid rejection — [tenderbot.kz](https://tenderbot.kz/blog/post/tipichnye-oshibki-pri-uchastii-v-tenderakh-i-kak-ikh-izbezhat)
- Domestic producers are exempt from all types of security under the new law (also lower penalties, minimum delivery term raised from 15 to 60 calendar days, payment term cut from 30 to 10 working days) — [bizmedia.kz, 17 Apr 2025](https://bizmedia.kz/2025-04-17-zakon-o-goszakupkah-v-kazahstane-kak-on-otrazitsya-na-biznese-i-podryadchikah/)
- Dumping as a competitive pain: a regional Atameken discussion (article "Госзакупки под срывом", date not visible; the low article ID in the URL suggests an old publication) describes ex-employees registering as IEs and one-day firms without experience or staff submitting ill-considered price offers, then re-registering under another name — [diapazon.kz](https://diapazon.kz/news/8949-goszakupki-pod-sryvom)
- March 2026 Minfin draft would have prohibited dumping offers outright; deputy Aituar Koshmambetov called this, together with the tax threshold, discriminatory and price-raising — [kz.kursiv.media](https://kz.kursiv.media/2026-03-18/fvfv-deputaty-protiv-popravok-kotorye-zakroyut-300-tys-kompaniy-dostup-k-goszakupkam/); [orda.kz](https://orda.kz/malyj-biznes-pod-ugrozoj-deputaty-pozhalovalis-bektenovu-na-novye-pravila-zakupok-413903/)

### Inferences
- For a small supplier the cash cost is cumulative: annual portal tariff + bid security + performance security + possibly an anti-dumping sum, all held until full performance, followed by a payment wait. This is a money problem, not a software problem; software can only compute the exposure before bidding and remind about posting deadlines.
- Because dumping thresholds differ by method (and changed on 1 Jan 2026), a "dumping calculator" that applies the right rule per lot type addresses a real confusion (MCFR even publishes one).

### Gaps
- Standard percentages of bid security and performance security under the 2026 Rules, and the total amounts frozen on e-wallets, could not be confirmed (query blocked by the search budget).
- No data on how often security is returned late, or on typical delay length.
- No supplier quotes on bank-guarantee problems were found.

## Register of Unscrupulous Participants (реестр недобросовестных участников, РНУ): how suppliers end up in it, how many, consequences

### Takeaway
The register held roughly 17.8–18.0 thousand records in autumn 2026, and courts satisfied 13,410 claims to recognise suppliers as unscrupulous in 2025 (23,114 in 2024). Inclusion bars the supplier from public procurement for 24 months; grounds are evasion from signing the contract, false information, and non- or improper performance.

### Cited Findings
- Register size: 17,981 records as of 24 Sept 2026 (from zakup.gov.kz) — [duna.kz](https://duna.kz/research/kazakhstan/b2b-prodazhi-i-tendery-goszakupki-kazakhstan-2026); the register page itself showed «Найдено записей: 17 783» at the time it was indexed — [zakup.gov.kz/registry/rnus](https://zakup.gov.kz/registry/rnus)
- Earlier figure (publication date not visible in the search output; clearly older than the 2026 count): 12,661 potential suppliers in the register per Sabit Akhmetov, director of the procurement legislation department of the Ministry of Finance; term in the register is two years; exclusion by court decision or expiry — [informburo.kz](https://informburo.kz/novosti/bolee-125-tysyachi-kazahstanskih-kompaniy-popali-v-reestr-nedobrosovestnyh-postavshchikov-.html)
- Court flow (Supreme Court data): 2024 — 29,150 decisions, 23,114 claims satisfied; 2025 — 17,626 decisions, 13,410 satisfied; the number of cases on unscrupulous suppliers fell to 22,592 in 2025 (−41.3%); regional growth in Mangystau (1,180 → 1,212) and Turkistan (466 → 638). «При этом снижение общего количества судебных споров само по себе не свидетельствует об уменьшении числа нарушений при государственных закупках.» (fragment) — [rus.baq.kz](https://rus.baq.kz/podryadchikov-spasali-ot-chernogo-spiska-chto-vyyavil-verhovnyy-sud-v-goszakupkah-320053509/). The same article cites a different "Төрелік" figure for 2024 (38,500 claims and cases); coverage differs and is not explained.
- Supreme Court position quoted in the same article: «Для удовлетворения иска о признании Поставщика недобросовестным участником закупок недостаточно наличия лишь одного факта нарушения им обязательства. Нарушения должны быть признаны судом существенными, повлекшими негативные последствия для Заказчика» (fragment) — [rus.baq.kz](https://rus.baq.kz/podryadchikov-spasali-ot-chernogo-spiska-chto-vyyavil-verhovnyy-sud-v-goszakupkah-320053509/)
- Consequence: suppliers in the register are not admitted to public procurement for 24 months; after 24 months the record is removed automatically — [dknews.kz](https://dknews.kz/ru/biznes/403477-goszakupki-kazahstana-odin-sryv-mozhet-zakryt-rynok); [gz.mcfr.kz](https://gz.mcfr.kz/article/797-reestr-nedobrosovestnyh-postavshchikov-goszakupok). Sources differ on the start of the term: from entry into force of the court decision ([gz.mcfr.kz](https://gz.mcfr.kz/article/934-isklyuchenie-iz-reestra-nedobrosovestnyh)) vs. from the date of inclusion ([gos24.kz](https://gos24.kz/blog/270/)).
- Grounds (as restated for the quasi-state rules from 12 July 2026): evasion from concluding the contract, providing false information, improper performance of obligations — [zakon.kz, 15 May 2026](https://www.zakon.kz/pravo/6517963-izmenilis-pravila-goszakupok-predstavitelyami-kvizigosudarstvennogo-sektora.html)
- East Kazakhstan, 2026 year-to-date: 599 claims and cases on procurement reached the economic court, 428 of them about performance of concluded contracts (missed delivery dates, penalties) — [inform.kz](https://www.inform.kz/ru/599-iskov-iz-za-chego-predprinimateli-sudyatsya-pogoszakupkam-vvko-fa58bb2b); [factum.kz](https://factum.kz/2026/131811/); the business ombudsman's office reported more than 800 court acts recognising suppliers as unscrupulous in East Kazakhstan — [ombudsmanbiz.kz](https://ombudsmanbiz.kz/rus/press-centr/news/?cid=0&rid=1582)
- Petition on Epetition.kz asks to revise how the register is formed and applied, arguing that inclusion effectively restricts the constitutional right to work and to entrepreneurial activity — [kapital.kz](https://kapital.kz/economic/136837/peresmotret-podhod-k-reestru-nedobrosovestnyh-uchastnikov-goszakupok-predlagayut-v-rk.html)
- Supplier voice (forum thread title/post, date not visible): «На мое ИП, которое входит в реестр недобросовестных поставщиков подали иск из-за не поставки товара по выигранному тендеру.» (fragment) — [forum.zakon.kz, court cases on public procurement](https://forum.zakon.kz/forum/335-%D1%81%D1%83%D0%B4%D0%B5%D0%B1%D0%BD%D1%8B%D0%B5-%D0%B4%D0%B5%D0%BB%D0%B0-%D0%BF%D0%BE-%D0%B3%D0%BE%D1%81%D1%83%D0%B4%D0%B0%D1%80%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B2%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%BD%D1%8B%D0%BC-%D0%B7%D0%B0%D0%BA%D1%83%D0%BF%D0%BA%D0%B0%D0%BC/)
- Rating-point system scores take register presence into account in 2026 — [femida-justice.com](https://femida-justice.com/yuridicheskie-uslugi-almaty/goszakupki-po-novomu-kak-rabotat-po-zakonu-2025-goda/)
- Samruk-Kazyna keeps its own list of unreliable suppliers (expanded from 1 Jan 2026); sources disagree on the term: 6–24 months depending on severity ([femida-justice.com](https://femida-justice.com/blog/reestr-nedobrosovestnyh/)) vs. 24 months standard ([sensus.kz](https://sensus.kz/journal/reestr-nedobrosovestnyh-postavshchikov-samruk-kazyna)); changes described at [tenderbot.kz](https://tenderbot.kz/blog/post/izmeneniya-v-poryadke-zakupok-ao-samrukazyna)
- Counter-initiative on the supplier side of the ledger: a register of unscrupulous customers has been announced — [inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/ru/last/v-kazahstane-vnedryat-reestr-nedobrosovestnyh-zakazchikov)

### Inferences
- With ~120 thousand active suppliers and ~18 thousand live register records (a two-year stock), the blacklist touches on the order of one in seven active suppliers at any time — a rough ratio, since records can include repeat entries and inactive firms.
- The paths into the register that software can help avoid are the procedural ones: missing the contract-signing window, missing the security-posting deadline, and missing a delivery date that was visible in the contract. Reminders and execution tracking are relevant; the 24-month sanction itself is law.
- The Supreme Court's "one violation is not enough, it must be material" position gives suppliers an argument; drafting an objection that documents immateriality is a document task.

### Gaps
- No official count of inclusions per year split by ground (evasion from signing vs. non-performance vs. false data) was found; court-satisfied claims (13,410 in 2025) are the nearest proxy and exclude inclusions made without a court decision.
- Petition text, author and number of signatures were not retrieved.
- Date of the 12,661 figure is not confirmed.

## Payment delays, penalties (неустойка), contract execution problems, acceptance acts

### Takeaway
Late payment by state bodies is described by Atameken as one of the most painful problems for SMEs (frozen working capital, trouble servicing loans), and most procurement court disputes in a sample region concern contract execution. A new pre-payment check of acceptance acts (from 1 July 2026) has added a fresh source of anxiety; penalties run at 0.1% per day in both directions, but in practice the customer withholds while the supplier must sue.

### Inferences
- Of the delay causes listed by MCFR, two are within the supplier's control (documents submitted late, missing ЭСФ) and are checklist/reminder problems; the rest (no financing, no balance) are structural.

## What changed with the new Law (from 1 Jan 2025), the 2026 rule changes and the Unified Procurement Platform; rating-point system, financial stability, tax-payment and experience requirements

### Takeaway
The reform replaced commissions with automated scoring for a growing set of works (faster, but with no pre-trial appeal), moved complaints to the organiser, and tied eligibility ever more tightly to tax and experience data held in state systems. In 2026 suppliers then faced at least six rule amendments, a mid-year tariff change applied retroactively, a contested draft that would have required VAT-payer status and banned dumping, and a move of login/registration to zakup.gov.kz on 31 Aug 2026 that began with authorisation failures.

### Cited Findings
**New law, 2025**
- Rating-point system: winner determined automatically from state data, no tender commission, results not appealable, contest takes 5 days instead of 30 (zakon.kz: about 29 → 5 working days); initially construction, design and technical supervision, with medium repair and landscaping planned from 1 Sept 2025; main criteria are experience, financial stability, regional affiliation; points for experience, tax payments, absence of delays — [bizmedia.kz, 17 Apr 2025](https://bizmedia.kz/2025-04-17-zakon-o-goszakupkah-v-kazahstane-kak-on-otrazitsya-na-biznese-i-podryadchikah/); [zakon.kz, 10 Feb 2025](https://www.zakon.kz/ekonomika-biznes/6466544-novye-pravila-goszakupok-v-kazakhstane-chto-vazhno-znat.html)
- In 2026 building-modernisation works with design estimates and gasification are procured through the rating-point system without a commission — [gz.mcfr.kz](https://gz.mcfr.kz/article/1623-modernizaciya-osnashcheniya-zdaniya-v-goszakupkah-rk-v-2026-godu-novye-pravila-i-trebovaniya); [gz.mcfr.kz](https://gz.mcfr.kz/article/1678-gazifikaciya-v-goszakupkah-rk-kak-provesti-tender-i-ustanovit-trebovaniya-k-podryadchiku-v-2026-godu)
- New bans: suppliers whose head or founder committed corruption offences, and suppliers with overdue wage arrears, are not admitted — [akorda.kz](https://www.akorda.kz/ru/glavoy-gosudarstva-podpisan-zakon-respubliki-kazahstan-o-gosudarstvennyh-zakupkah-164114)
- Results claimed by Minfin: 2025 contracts totalled 8.1 trn tenge (+53% on 2024 per the article); contracts with domestic producers up 23% to 460 bn tenge — [inform.kz](https://www.inform.kz/ru/reforma-goszakupok-obem-dogovorov-s-kazahstanskimi-proizvoditelyami-viros-na-23-5572d1); [bizmedia.kz](https://bizmedia.kz/2026-01-28-kontrakty-s-otechestvennymi-proizvoditelyami-v-kazahstane-vyrosli-na-23/)
- Construction: one year into the reform, "turnkey" construction is called progressive but, per expert Gaukhar Kopzhasarova, not all customers and contractors understand the risk allocation and project-management requirements; a requirement was returned for certain works after seven years without it (details not visible) — [inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/ru/news/goszakupki-stroitelstva-pervye-itogi-reformy-i-spor-vokrug-trebovanij-k-podryadchikam)

**Financial stability and paid taxes**
- For a contest with preliminary qualification selection a supplier is financially stable if paid taxes are at least 3% of income, calculated over three years (ПУН = УН / СД × 100%); 1% for fuel purchases and industrial-assembly agreement holders; taxes count in the year they actually reach the budget; small business is checked without the fixed-assets indicator — [pvl.kgd.gov.kz](https://pvl.kgd.gov.kz/ru/news/trebovaniya-po-finansovoy-ustoychivosti-dlya-uchastiya-v-goszakupkah-15-71127); [tenderplus.kz](https://tenderplus.kz/blog/finansovaya-ustoychivost-postavshchika-v-gosudarstvennykh-zakupkakh); [online.zakon.kz](https://online.zakon.kz/Document/?doc_id=34011732)
- A law firm claims the threshold rose to 3.5% from 2026 in connection with VAT going to 16% — [femida-justice.com](https://femida-justice.com/uslugi/yurist-po-tenderam-almaty/finansovaya-ustojchivost-2026-nds-16-i-novyie-nalogi/) (not confirmed by an official source)
- Real case: errors in a company's tax reporting kept it out of procurement in 2025; after it filed corrected declarations the financial-stability indicators on the portal were not updated, although it had repeatedly won procurements from 2022 to February 2025; the court held that only the Ministry of Finance can correct the data on the portal and that the State Revenue Committee supplies it — [inbusiness.kz, "По следам одной госзакупки…"](https://inbusiness.kz/ru/author_news/po-sledam-odnoj-goszakupki%E2%80%A6)

**Experience**
- Experience counts only for contracts performed within public procurement or recorded in state information systems, and only if shown in the experience register with status «Подтверждено»; subcontractor experience is not counted; in construction one point per completed object per year, maximum five; no experience requirement where the service/work needs a permit or notification — [adilet.zan.kz, Rules](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2400035238); [pro1c.kz](https://pro1c.kz/articles/prochee/pravila-gosudarstvennykh-zakupok/)

**2026 amendments and the contested draft**

### Inferences
- Eligibility now depends on data the supplier does not control on the portal (tax indicators from the revenue committee, experience register status). The inbusiness court case shows that a reporting error can lock a previously successful supplier out for a year with no quick correction path. Pre-bid self-diagnosis of these indicators is software-addressable; the correction itself is not.
- Counting only the items found, 2026 brought amendments effective 1 Jan, 27 Feb, 12 Jul, 1 Aug, 12 Sep (plus a 22 Jul update), a tariff order on 26 Jun, act control from 1 Jul and the portal switch on 31 Aug. For a small supplier without a dedicated specialist, simply knowing which rule applies to a given lot is a burden — the strongest case in this research for an assistant that tracks and explains changes.
- The status of the VAT-payer / dumping-ban / "any amount" financial-stability draft is unclear: order No. 297 did change financial-stability criteria, but whether the contested provisions were adopted, softened or dropped was not confirmed.

### Gaps
- Final fate of the March 2026 draft (VAT-payer requirement, dumping ban, 8% tax-burden threshold) not confirmed.
- Exact content of the changed points of the Rules (57, 198, 220, 379, 452, 460, 461, 545) not retrieved.
- No supplier quotes about the zakup.gov.kz interface or about confusion between platforms were found; evidence of burden is limited to official outage notices and the density of amendments.
- Current scoring formula of the rating-point system (weights for experience, taxes, region) and supplier complaints about it were not retrieved.

## Barriers for beginners: ЭЦП, registration, portal fees, procurement methods, learning curve

### Takeaway
Hard evidence exists for the money barriers (an annual portal tariff from 3,170 to 386,683 tenge depending on bid size, e-shop commission up to 40 MRP per sale) and for the experience barrier (contests are "practically pointless" without confirmed experience); evidence on ЭЦП and registration friction is thin and comes only from service guides.

### Cited Findings
- Portal tariffs (Minfin order No. 390 of 10 June 2026, in force 26 June 2026, applied to relations from 1 Jan 2026; operator — АО «Центр электронных финансов»): annual fee by the maximum bid/contract amount — up to 1 mln tenge: 3,170; up to 10 mln: 31,695; up to 100 mln: 63,391; up to 1 bn: 190,172; above 1 bn: 386,683 tenge; excluding VAT; access valid 12 months from payment; tariffs previously in force for 2026 lapsed on 26 June — [inform.kz](https://www.inform.kz/ru/novie-tarifi-nadostup-kportalu-goszakupok-utverdili-vkazahstane-a7d93c22); [sozmedia.kz](https://sozmedia.kz/124743/); [gos24.kz](https://gos24.kz/news/8684); [gos24.kz](https://gos24.kz/news/8728); [gz.mcfr.kz](https://gz.mcfr.kz/news/4027-novye-tarify-na-veb-portale-goszakupok-v-2026-godu-poshagovaya-instrukciya-dlya-postavshchikov); [goszakup.gov.kz news](https://goszakup.gov.kz/ru/news/view/1622)
- Other portals charge separately (the National Bank's procurement portal sets supplier tariffs in MRP per year) — [zakup.nationalbank.kz](https://zakup.nationalbank.kz/ru/pages/show/supplier_tariff)
- E-shop (Omarket): base commission 5% of the goods' value, capped at 40 MRP (173,000 tenge in 2026) per MCFR; orda.kz (about Dec 2025) reports 3% for domestic producers and 5% for foreign goods; the supplier pays the commission on each sale; activist Bibek Zhunusov demanded abandoning Omarket because a private company earns a commission on every state purchase; e-shop purchases are for goods up to 4,000 MRP — [gz.mcfr.kz](https://gz.mcfr.kz/article/1285-kak-provodit-zakupki-v-elektronnom-magazine-omarketkz); [orda.kz](https://orda.kz/v-omarketkz-otvetili-na-pretenzii-afm-408406/)
- Experience barrier, as phrased by a tender service: «если у вашей компании нет подтвержденного опыта (актов выполненных работ за последние пару лет), участвовать в конкурсе практически бессмысленно» (fragment); request for quotations: «Сложность: Низкая. Обычно не требуется подтверждение опыта работы.» (fragment) — [tenderbot.kz](https://tenderbot.kz/blog/post/kak-vybrat-format-zakupki-dlya-pervyh-pobed)
- ЭЦП / software: guides tell suppliers to keep a current NCALayer (from pki.gov.kz) and to bookmark the new address; lack of an ЭЦП signature where required and wrong file names are listed among rejection causes — [tenderbot.kz](https://tenderbot.kz/blog/post/novyy-portal-goszakupok-zakup-gov-kz); [tenderbot.kz](https://tenderbot.kz/blog/post/tipichnye-oshibki-pri-uchastii-v-tenderakh-i-kak-ikh-izbezhat)
- Methods to learn: five procurement methods with 2026 thresholds are described in service guides — [obf.kz](https://obf.kz/tendery/pravila/sposoby-zakupok/); [mytender.kz](https://mytender.kz/guide/sposoby-goszakupok)
- Legal-form trap: some lots need licences only legal entities can hold — [gz.mcfr.kz](https://gz.mcfr.kz/article/1411-too-ili-ip-chto-luchshe-vybrat-dlya-uchastiya-v-goszakupkah-rk)
- Low participation: about 5.9% of active legal entities and IEs take part in public procurement (duna.kz estimate) — [duna.kz](https://duna.kz/research/kazakhstan/b2b-prodazhi-i-tendery-goszakupki-kazakhstan-2026)

### Inferences
- A beginner bidding on lots up to 10 mln tenge pays 31,695 tenge a year before the first bid, then security, then (in the e-shop) up to 5% commission. For micro-suppliers the entry cost is noticeable but not prohibitive; the experience and tax-indicator rules are the stronger filter.
- A new supplier's realistic path is price-only methods (request for quotations, e-shop) — exactly where dumping by one-day firms is reported. That combination (only price methods are open, and they are a race to the bottom) is structural.
- The onboarding content published by every tender service (guides on methods, ЭЦП, first wins) indicates demand for guided onboarding, but this is indirect evidence.

### Gaps
- No evidence was found of supplier protest against the June 2026 tariffs, and the previous tariff levels (to quantify the change) were not retrieved.
- No data on time needed to register, obtain ЭЦП or complete a first application.
- No direct beginner testimonies were retrieved.

## How much time does a small business spend per application, and how do they currently solve it?

### Takeaway
No reliable figure for hours per application was found. What is visible is the outsourcing market: one-off preparation of tender documents is advertised from about 10–15 thousand to 150 thousand tenge, and full support subscriptions run from hundreds of thousands to about 2 mln tenge.

- Law firms sell tender support and rejected-bid appeals as services — [sdlegalpro.kz](https://sdlegalpro.kz/tendery-goszakupki-kazakhstan/)

### Inferences
- Price points give a rough willingness-to-pay anchor: tens of thousands of tenge per application for one-off help, roughly 270 thousand tenge a month for a managed service (800,000 per quarter). An assistant priced well below one outsourced application per month would be competing with freelancers on Kaspi rather than with managed services.
- The spread from 10,000 to 150,000 tenge per document set implies large variation in application complexity (request for quotations vs. contest with qualification).

### Gaps
- Hours per application: not found. No survey or vendor claim was retrieved (query blocked by search budget).
- Share of suppliers using an in-house specialist vs. outsourcing vs. the owner doing it: not found.
- Salary levels of in-house tender specialists were not researched.

## What do suppliers explicitly ask for (forums, chats, surveys, appeals to Atameken)?

### Takeaway
The explicit asks on record are institutional rather than individual: enforce payment and security-return deadlines, stop formal rejections and restrictive specs, soften the blacklist, assess regulatory impact before changing rules, and make act-control criteria specific. First-hand supplier posts from chats and forums were largely out of reach in this session.

### Cited Findings
- Atameken council (Sept 2026): recommended that the General Prosecutor's Office strengthen oversight of compliance with payment deadlines and return of security; said cases where courts side with business point to organisational problems in procurement control — [inbusiness.kz](https://inbusiness.kz/ru/last/atameken-nazval-glavnye-problemy-goszakupok-dlya-biznesa)

### Inferences
- None of the recorded asks is "give us software": suppliers ask the state for predictability, payment discipline and fair evaluation. - The asks that map to software are implicit: specific, checkable criteria (what exactly will be checked in an act; what exactly disqualifies a bid) are requests for clarity that an assistant can partly supply by reading the documents.
